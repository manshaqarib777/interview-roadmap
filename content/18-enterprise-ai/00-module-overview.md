# Module 18 — Enterprise AI Solutions Architecture

## Why this module comes eighteenth

Modules 7–17 built, shipped, secured, measured, and designed the AI product: the model decision (M18), the app (M19), the knowledge (M20), the loop (M21), the automation (M22), the backend (M23), the cloud (M24), the pipeline (M25), the security (M26), the observability (M27), and the design (M28). Every one of them is **run in an enterprise** — and this module is the enterprise. The L358 design is the city; this module is **the company that runs it**: the requirements (L359), the stakeholders (L360), the ADRs (L361), the technology and the vendor selection (L362–366), the trade-offs (L367), the cost and the capacity (L368–369), the scalability (L370), the compliance (L371), the governance (L372–373), the DR (L374), the integrations (L375–376), the multi-cloud (L377), the platform (L378), the case study (L379), and the architect's toolkit (L380).

The distinction this module is built on: a **specialist** knows the technology. A **solutions architect** runs the enterprise (L380) — the ADRs (L361), the cost models (L368), and the decision rule (L380) — the deliverables (L380) that the business signs.

## How to study each lesson

1. **Run the enterprise loop (L380).** The requirements (L359) → the stakeholders (L360) → the ADR (L361) → the selection (L362–366) → the trade-offs (L367) → the cost (L368) → the capacity (L369) → the governance (L373) — the deliverables (L380) the enterprise signs.
2. **Learn the enterprise vocabulary.** The ADRs (L361), the build vs buy (L363), the vendor (L364), the compliance (L371), the governance (L373) — the words every enterprise discussion uses.
3. **Apply the earlier modules.** The L358 design (L358) is what the enterprise selects (L362); the L285 cost (L285) is what the enterprise budgets (L368); the L325 security (L325) is what the enterprise complies (L371). This module is the earlier modules, enterprise-run (L380).
4. **Build the capstone at the end (L380).** The final lesson assembles the toolkit (L380) — the ADRs (L361), the diagrams (L380), the cost models (L368), and the decision rule (L380) — the deliverables (L380) of the architect. Draw them, defend them, and M29 is claimed.
