## 2. Mental Model

Think of the ECR as **the warehouse with the numbered shelves.** The warehouse (the ECR, L294) stores the crates (the images, L288) on the labeled shelves (the repositories, L294): the `ai-service` shelf (L294), the `worker` shelf (L294). Each crate has the labels (the tags, L294): the version — `1.2.3` — and the batch number — `abc1234` (L291) — and the labels are locked (the immutability, L294): a crate can't be relabeled (L294). The delivery trucks (the CI, L296) drop off the new crates (the pushes, L294), and the stores (the ECS, L295) order the crates by the labels (the pulls, L294). The warehouse's inspectors (the scans, L293) check each crate for the damage (the vulnerabilities, L293) and flag the findings (L293). And the warehouse's manager (the lifecycle, L294) clears the old crates (the retention, L294) — the shelves stay lean (L294).

- **The scans (L293).** The ECR scans the image (L294) for the vulnerabilities (L293) — the findings (L293) surfaced (L294), the CI (L296) gated (L293).
- **The lifecycle (L294).** The retention policies (L294) — the old tags (L294) cleaned up (L294) — the repo lean (L294).

> [!NOTE]
