## 4. How It Works — The Store, Part by Part

- **The buckets (L265).** The containers with globally unique names (L265). The bucket is the security and the cost boundary (L265): the policy on the bucket, the tier on the object (L265).
- **The keys (L265).** The object names inside the bucket (L265). The key is the layout's design (L265): `tenant/{id}/docs/...` — the isolation (L320) and the lifecycle (L265) by the prefix (L265).
- **The storage classes (L265).** The tiers: the Standard (the hot data), the Infrequent Access (the cold, with a retrieval fee), the Glacier (the archive, minutes to hours to retrieve) (L265). The lifecycle rules (L265) move the objects automatically (L265).
- **The security (L262).** The bucket policies — what the bucket allows (L265); the IAM (L262) — who may act; the encryption (L275) — at rest and in transit (L265). The public bucket is the breach (L265).
- **The versioning (L265).** The object's history (L265) — the accidental overwrite undone (L265), the audit's evidence (L322).

> [!NOTE]
> **The bucket policy and the IAM are different layers (L262, L265).** The IAM (L262) says who may act — the Lambda's role (L262) may read the bucket (L265). The bucket policy says what the bucket allows — the public read for the frontend (L272), the deny for the rest (L265). The senior design uses both: the IAM for the identities (L262), the bucket policy for the bucket's boundary (L265) — the L172 baseline, storage-shaped (L265).

## 8. Common Mistakes

- **The public bucket (L265).** The data readable by the internet (L265) — the L312 leakage (L265).
- **The single class (L265).** Everything in the Standard (L265) — the cold data's cost (L285) ignored.
- **The keys without the layout (L265).** The flat keys (L265) — the isolation (L320) and the lifecycle (L265) impossible.
- **The versioning off (L265).** The overwrite unrecoverable (L265) — the recovery (L304) lost.
- **The credentials in the app (L265).** The keys in the code (L265) — the roles (L262) are the access (L262).

## 9. Best Practices

- **Keep the buckets private** (L265) — the access explicit (L262).
- **Design the keys** (L265) — the tenant (L320), the prefix, the lifecycle (L265).
- **Tier by the lifecycle** (L265) — the hot fast, the cold cheap (L285).
- **Turn on the versioning** (L265) — the recovery (L304) and the audit (L322).
- **Encrypt at rest** (L275) — the data at rest protected (L265).
